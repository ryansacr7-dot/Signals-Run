PRIVACY POLICY

Signal Run: Ghosts Journey · Last updated 10 October 2026

Signal Run does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, no accounts and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This includes:

  • Your high score, level, rank and in-game currency
  • Guns, attachments and skins you have unlocked
  • Your chosen pilot name and settings
  • Daily task progress and achievements

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, it is not readable by us, and it is not shared with anyone. Deleting the app removes it.

Internet access

The game makes no network requests. Once installed, it runs entirely offline.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no social media integrations, no cloud services.

Children

Because the game collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. It is safe for players of any age in this respect.

Your rights

There is no data for us to show you, correct, export or delete, because we never receive any. The save data on your device is entirely under your control, and the game includes a Reset Progress option that erases it.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
