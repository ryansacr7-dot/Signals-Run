Common questions

How do I move and shoot?

Drag the stick on the right to move. Drag the stick on the left to aim and fire. Turn on AUTO in the top right and the ship fires at the nearest enemy by itself.

What is the dash button?

A short burst of speed that makes you briefly hard to hit. It has a cooldown, shown by the heat bar above it.

How do I get new guns?

Earn Imanra by playing, then buy guns in Loadout → Gun Shop. There are seven in total.

How do attachments work?

Attachments change how a gun behaves — bouncing shots, explosive rounds, chain lightning and more. You start with one slot. Own every gun to unlock a second, and every attachment to unlock a third.

Will I lose my progress?

Your save lives on your device. Deleting the app erases it. Use Backup & Restore in the settings to copy your save to another device.

The game feels stuck or looks wrong.

Close the app fully and reopen it. If a problem persists, email me with your device model and what you were doing, and I'll fix it.

Reporting a bug

The more detail the better: what happened, what you expected, which mode you were in, and a screenshot if you have one.
